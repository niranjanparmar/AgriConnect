Empowering Indian Agriculture

Connecting Harvests Directly to Kitchens

AgriConnect is India's digital agricultural marketplace and wholesale price intelligence platform. We eliminate multi-tier commission intermediaries, connecting sustainable smallholder farmers directly with household consumers, wholesale buyers, and local markets.

By integrating verified producer listings with daily open-market APMC mandi pricing from the Ministry of Agriculture, we give farmers the tools to market their harvests at fair value with complete pricing transparency.

Our Mission & Vision

Democratize agricultural commerce across India by ensuring producers receive equitable returns for their labor, while consumers enjoy fresh, traceable, farm-gate produce.
